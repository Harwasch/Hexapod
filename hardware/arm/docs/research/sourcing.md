# FA-1 actuator sourcing outside US retail (checked 2026-10-17)

## Bottom line

1. **The "US retail" baseline is not landed cost.** aifitlab.com, the source of most current BOM prices, ships from China by SF Express. Its list prices exclude duties ([aifitlab shipping policy](https://aifitlab.com/policies/shipping-policy)). Robotopian also fulfils mostly from Shenzhen ([policy](https://robotopian.com/policies/shipping-policy)). Hiwonder ships FOB Shenzhen ([policy](https://www.hiwonder.com/pages/shipping)). Every China-origin motor pays about **40.3% US duty** on arrival (see Tariffs). So the true landed cost of the current retail BOM is about **1.40 × list + shipping**.
2. **The cheapest credible channels:**
   - **DDP resellers.** These sellers include duties in the sticker price. [OpenELAB US](https://openelab.com/policies/shipping-policy) says "Import duties and customs clearance fees are included in the price" and ships free over $55. [Zennixtek](https://www.zennixtek.com/policies/shipping-policy) says it "provides Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) shipping". Their sticker prices are close to aifitlab's ex-duty prices, so they come out **20–30% cheaper landed**.
   - **Taobao via an agent, at RobStride's official CNY prices** ([RobStride Product_Information README, spec dated 2026-07-13](https://github.com/RobStride/Product_Information)). These prices are 30–45% below export prices before duty. After duty, agent fee and freight they land 26–40% below retail landed, roughly level with the DDP resellers.
3. **Channels that did not help:**
   - **AliExpress** is _more_ expensive than the resellers for every QDD actuator checked: RobStride, Damiao and Steadywin listings are 0–100% higher. AliExpress US also adds the import charge at checkout (`taxRate` 0.403 on most motor listings). AliExpress wins only for the **Feetech STS3215** ($12.81 vs $26).
   - **Steadywin factory direct** ([steadywin-motor.com](https://steadywin-motor.com/products.json)) is _not_ cheaper than resellers for complete actuators. GIM6010-8+GDS68+2nd encoder costs $105.65 there vs $105 at aifitlab, and GIM8108-8+GDS68+2ES68 costs $140.95 vs $135. It is much cheaper for the **housing-less GWK cores**: GWK8108-8 $70.36, GWK6010-8 $46.82.
4. **FA-1 arm-set impact** (yaw EduLite 05, shoulder GIM8108-8, elbow GIM6010-8, HX-35HM, HX-65HM, STS3215):
   - Retail list: **$432**. Estimated landed: **$635**.
   - Cheapest credible landed: **≈ $507** (−$128 per arm, −20%), with the same parts and the same variants. The savings come mainly from EduLite on Taobao (−$47), GIM8108-8 at OpenELAB DDP (−$39), GIM6010-8 at OpenELAB DDP (−$24) and STS3215 on AliExpress (−$18).
   - Compared on list prices only, the gain looks like ~$0, because the cheaper options are DDP or duty-inclusive. **Compare on landed cost.**
5. **Driver deprecation risk.** Steadywin says GDS/GDZ boards "will be phased out subsequently" and is moving to GDK boards ([GIM6010-8 page](https://steadywin-motor.com/products/built-in-star-gear-motor-motor-robot-joint-driver-actuator-c)). GDK variants cost $15–30 more. Buy all GDS68 units for the 10-arm run in one batch.

- **Total: ≈ 40.3% of declared value** for these motors. This matches the 0.403 import-charge rate AliExpress US shows on most motor listings today, and 39.9% on Feetech servo listings.
- **Other fees.** Express carriers add brokerage or disbursement fees on DDU parcels, typically $10–50 per shipment (estimate). An order over $2,500 (≈ 30 QDDs) needs a formal entry with a customs bond, which means a broker. Do not split shipments to dodge this.
- **Watch the 2026-11-10 expiry** of the 2025 US–China truce and the extended 301 exclusions. Rates may change.
- This is not customs advice. Confirm the HTS line with the carrier or broker.

## Cost model used for "landed" (estimates are marked)

- **FX: 6.8 CNY/USD** (estimate). The last dated quote found was 6.77 on 2026-07-26 ([pluang](https://pluang.com/en/tools/currency-converter/usd-cny), [poundsterlinglive](https://poundsterlinglive.com/data/currencies/usd-pairs/USDCNY-exchange-rate)).
- **Taobao via agent:**
  - Item = CNY/6.8 × 1.05 + $1 (estimate: ~5% agent service fee plus warehouse handling and domestic post).
  - Freight = **$15 per packed kg** by consolidated DHL/FedEx (estimate). Forwarder quotes run $12.5–40/kg ([sino-shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/da/fragtpriser-fra-kina/express-omkostninger/dhl-fedex-ups-prissammenligning-kina/), [goodhopefreight](https://goodhopefreight.com/dhl/usa-list.html)), and motors carry magnets, so ask the agent for a "sensitive goods" line.
  - Duty = 40.3% × CNY/6.8, with goods declared at the true value.
- **DDU sellers** (aifitlab, Steadywin direct, Hiwonder, AliExpress): landed = price × 1.403 + freight. Freight is estimated at the same $15/kg; Hiwonder ships free over $499.
- **DDP sellers:** landed = price + freight. OpenELAB freight is $0 over $55. Zennixtek freight is calculated at checkout, so the same $15/kg estimate is used.
- **Quantity pricing.** None of these listings show 10-unit tiers except Seeed (EduLite $76 at 10+) and SMC Powers (6+/12+). Ask OpenELAB, Zennixtek and Steadywin for a 30-unit quote.

## Agent / Taobao practicalities

- RobStride lists official domestic prices on its own GitHub, so a Taobao agent order should match them. Order through an agent (e.g. Superbuy, CSSBuy) that offers DHL/FedEx/UPS. Declare the true value, because duty is collected at the door.
- **Steadywin and Damiao domestic prices are much harder to verify.** The only data points are the dated GIM6010-8 ¥399/¥449 (CSDN 2024) and a DM-J4310-2EC ¥599 education price (Zhihu table updated Oct 2025, page blocked; seen in a search snippet). Message the agent for a quote before relying on them.
- Allow 2–4 weeks: about 1 week to the agent warehouse plus 1–2 weeks by express. Magnets may force a "sensitive" line.
