FA-2 Field Arm

A low-cost arm for the medium hexapod. It weeds, doses, samples, picks fruit, turns valves and fetches things. Its geometry and every part were chosen by optimizing against 17 field tasks at delivered prices, and the result was checked in physics simulation with the hexapod's own legs doing part of the work.

Trade study

How the design was chosen

The second study priced every part as delivered to the US (40.3% duty on Chinese motors plus freight) and opened three cost levers. The first is duty-paid resellers. The second is printed Dyneema capstan reductions on the turret motors. The third is bare motors run by our own driver board. Every price was checked against a live cart. It also let the body lean forward over planted feet and dropped poses the legs cannot hold. It required the base joints to turn fast enough for a 0.5 s dosing cycle and the tool roll to turn a 3 Nm valve. The same 1,764 geometries were swept, and the frontier geometries were tried with every assignment of parts under each lever setting.

Three ways to spend it

FA-1 re-scored under the same rules, and three picks from the new field-grade frontier.

Cost against capability

Each dot is a field-grade design on its own geometry's frontier, all levers on. Capability is the weighted share of task points served. Field grade means a backdrivable, force-sensing shoulder and elbow and under 6 mm of open-loop backlash at the tool.

What each lever is worth

The best field-grade capability for each parts budget with one lever on at a time, and with all three. A lever that never moves its curve left or up bought nothing.

Elbow drive and degrees of freedom

The best field-grade capability each architecture reaches for a given parts cost. The parallelogram lets the shoulder and elbow motors share a pull's moment instead of stacking it on the shoulder.

Link lengths

Kinematic coverage (reach, approach, collisions, tip-over) across upper-arm and forearm lengths, at the chosen mount, pedestal and drive.

Against arms you can buy

Parts or list price per kilogram-metre of rated payload × reach (log scale). Vendors rate payload differently, so FA-2 is shown both ways: continuous at full stretch (conservative) and peak.

Bill of materials

Parts, about 10 units

Delivered to the US at about 10 units (October 2026). Duty-paid resellers are used where a cart check confirmed the price; otherwise the price is the list price plus 40.3% duty and freight. Printed parts are white ASA outside and PA12-CF near the motors. STEP, STL and GLB files are generated from the same parameters as the simulation.

Reproduce: cd hardware/arm && uv run python -m armdesign.optimize, then python -m armdesign.report select, python -m armdesign.sim, python -m armdesign.cad and python -m armdesign.report.

The host is a reference medium hexapod (12 kg, 0.56 × 0.30 m body); no hexapod has been designed yet. Prices are landed in the US and were checked on 2026-10-17. Tariffs can move; the US–China truce expires on 2026-11-10.
