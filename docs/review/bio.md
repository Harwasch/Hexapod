# Review — Bio-inspiration: what real hexapods do and what transfers

`bio` · requested 2026-09-02 · branch `claude/hexapod-robot-design-mt516g`

A study of the insect leg (coxa, trochanter, femur, tibia, tarsus; the slanted thorax-coxa axis, the coxa-trochanter power joint, the femur-tibia posture joint), the cockroach's ground reaction forces (every leg carries half the body weight, lateral and fore-aft forces 35-52 % of vertical, aimed at the coxa to minimise joint moments), tripod static stability, passive tarsus and spines, strain sensing in the cuticle, the Biewener size-posture scaling, and the crevice posture. Each mapped to our design: what we already have, what to add (compliant clawed foot, tibia elasticity, strain gauges, a flat posture), what not to copy (the slanted first axis), and one reframing: the mammal stance is nature's posture at our size, so it may be the cruise mode and the sprawl the terrain mode.
