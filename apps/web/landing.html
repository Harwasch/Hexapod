01

The fleet

Everything ships in a single container and runs on solar power. Nothing to fuel and nothing to plug in.

Base

A 20-foot container that is the fleet's home on site. It unfolds its own solar array and connects by satellite.

Solar
≈5.2 kW est.
Link
Satellite + mesh radio
Holds
2 carriers, 32 walkers, 2 drones

Walker

A dog-sized robot with six legs and two arms, built for slopes, brush and rough ground.

Body
0.76 m long
Arms
2 grippers, ≈0.55 m reach est.
Rear hitch
Basket, mapping mast or drone pad

Carrier

A six-wheel hauler on rocking suspension. It moves walkers, tools and whatever gets pulled.

Carries
16 folded walkers
Swaps to
Chipper, bin, cargo or sensor mast

Mapping drone

A fixed-wing mapper that takes off vertically from the base roof and flies the whole site.

120 m
Detail
≈3 cm per pixel est.

02

Capabilities

The same crew changes tools and tasks from job to job and from season to season.

Map

Fly a whole site and build a 3D model of the land and everything on it.

Inspect

Check panels, poles, pipelines, fences and slopes with cameras, thermal and lidar.

Clear

Cut back brush and fire fuel around homes, panels, pipes and power lines.

Remove

Pull weeds and invasive plants by the root, without herbicide.

Plant

Plant trees, grasses and cover crops exactly where the plan says.

Haul

Move tools, material and robots over rough ground.

Patrol

Walk and fly fences and perimeters on a schedule.

Monitor

Come back on a schedule to catch regrowth, damage and change.
